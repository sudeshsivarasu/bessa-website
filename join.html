Membership application

Apply for BESSA membership

One form for all individual and organisational categories. Takes about eight minutes. On submission you receive a reference number and the application is sent to the Membership Secretary for review at the next Council meeting.

Who can apply

Anyone who works, studies or has a professional interest in biomedical, clinical or medical engineering in South Africa, and organisations active in health technology. Fellows and Honorary Members are elected by nomination, not by application.

What you need

Your highest qualification, years of relevant experience, and (students) your current programme. Proof is requested only if Council asks for it. Fees are invoiced after approval; nothing is payable now.
