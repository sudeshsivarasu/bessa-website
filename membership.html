Membership

Seven categories, one application form

Membership is open to anyone who works, studies or has a professional interest in biomedical, clinical or medical engineering in South Africa, and to organisations active in health technology.

Benefits

  • Reduced SABEC registration fees: BESSA members pay 20% less, saving R1,625 as a delegate or R875 as a student at early-bird rates for SABEC 2027
  • A national professional network
  • Full IFMBE member benefits
  • Webinars and workshops
  • Mentorship for students and early-career members
  • Eligibility for BESSA awards and Fellowship
  • A voice in accreditation, regulation and policy
  • The BESSA newsletter

Indicative annual fees for 2027

To be confirmed by Council

  • Member R750
  • Early Career R500
  • Associate R560
  • Student R375
  • Affiliate R3,000 (small) · R7,500 (medium) · R15,000 (large)
  • Fellows and Honorary Members pay no dues

Fees are invoiced after approval; nothing is payable at application.

A BESSA membership at R750 costs less than the SABEC member discount. Join before 15 January 2027 to register at the early-bird member rate.

How to apply

  1. Apply online (about eight minutes). You receive a reference number immediately.
  2. The Membership Secretary acknowledges within five working days and confirms your category within ten.
  3. The Membership Committee decides at its next monthly meeting; you receive an approval letter and invoice.
  4. Membership starts when the fee is received. You then get your membership number, welcome pack and group invitations.

Questions

Write to BESSA@uct.ac.za. Fellows and Honorary Members are elected by nomination, not by application; ask the Secretariat for the nomination form.

Organisations wishing to join as Affiliates use the same form and name one representative.
