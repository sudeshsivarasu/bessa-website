Engineering better health for South Africa.

BESSA is the South African member society of the International Federation for Medical and Biological Engineering (IFMBE). Established in 1978, we unite biomedical, clinical and medical engineers across universities, the health system, industry, regulators and government.

What we do

Six goals, aligned with the IFMBE Strategic Plan

01

Represent

The voice of biomedical and clinical engineering nationally, and South Africa's voice in the IFMBE.

02

Advance knowledge

Conferences, webinars, publications and the Southern Africa Biomedical Engineering Conference (SABEC).

03

Develop the profession

Education, accreditation and professional recognition of biomedical and clinical engineers, working with the Engineering Council of South Africa (ECSA) and universities.

04

Collaborate

Bridges between academia, hospitals, industry, SAHPRA, the Department of Science, Technology and Innovation and the health system.

05

Guide policy and standards

Position papers and guidelines on professional practice, education, ethics and health technology.

06

Build a sustainable society

Transparent governance, a growing membership and a constitution ready for non-profit registration.

Membership

Why join BESSA

A national network

Biomedical and clinical engineers, clinicians, industry and regulators across South Africa, with regional representatives and divisions launching in 2027.

IFMBE member benefits

Reduced fees at IFMBE conferences including the World Congress, eligibility for IFMBE awards and working groups, and representation at the IFMBE General Assembly.

Reduced SABEC fees

Members pay 20% less for SABEC 2027: a saving of R1,625 as a delegate or R875 as a student at early-bird rates. Indicative 2027 fees from R375 (students) and R750 (members), to be confirmed by Council.

Flagship conference

SABEC 2027 — Biomedical Engineering Without Borders

The Southern Africa Biomedical Engineering Conference, co-hosted by BESSA and UCT MedTech and endorsed by the IFMBE, takes place on 11–12 March 2027 at the UCT Graduate School of Business, Breakwater Campus, V&A Waterfront, Cape Town. Ten tracks, a Young Investigator Competition and student scholarships.

Early-bird registration closes 15 January 2027. BESSA members register at the member rate.
