About BESSA

Established 1978, BESSA is South Africa's professional home for biomedical engineering, and the country's member society in the International Federation for Medical and Biological Engineering.

Our history and mission

BESSA grew out of the country's first academic biomedical engineering programme, founded at the UCT Medical School in 1972, and is housed today at the Biomedical Engineering Research Centre (BMERC) at the University of Cape Town. Through the IFMBE it is part of the International Union for Physical and Engineering Sciences in Medicine (IUPESM).

Mission. To encourage, support, represent and unify the biomedical engineering community of South Africa, and to advance the research, development, application and management of engineering and technology in medicine and biology for health and quality of life.

Research and innovation focus

  • Frugal biodesign and medical devices: affordable, accessible devices designed for Africa's needs.
  • Digital health and AI: technology that improves care delivery in South African communities.
  • Clinical engineering and health infrastructure: sustainable health technology management for hospitals and clinics.
  • Local manufacturing and regulatory capacity: quality systems (ISO 13485), SAHPRA readiness and the South African MedTech Master Plan.
  • Publications: supporting members to publish in national and international journals and the IFMBE Proceedings.

Constitution

BESSA is governed by its Constitution and Membership Rules (2026 revision), drafted for registration as a non-profit organisation under the Nonprofit Organisations Act and aligned with the IFMBE Constitution. The Annual General Meeting is held each year, ordinarily alongside SABEC. The Constitution and Membership Rules are available from the Secretariat at BESSA@uct.ac.za.

International connection

Part of the IFMBE family

The IFMBE, founded in Paris in 1959, is the global federation of national and transnational societies in medical and biological engineering. It is a non-governmental organisation in official relations with the World Health Organization and the United Nations, and with the IOMP forms the International Union for Physical and Engineering Sciences in Medicine (IUPESM). BESSA is its member society for South Africa.

Through the IFMBE

  • the triennial World Congress on Medical Physics and Biomedical Engineering
  • the flagship journal Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing (MBEC)
  • the IFMBE Proceedings series and IFMBE News
  • the Clinical Engineering Division and the Health Technology Assessment Division
  • Working Groups including the Low- and Middle-Income Countries (LMIC) Working Group
  • the Council of Societies

What IFMBE membership means for every BESSA member

  1. Reduced registration at IFMBE-endorsed conferences including the World Congress
  2. Eligibility for IFMBE awards, fellowships and early-career programmes
  3. Participation in IFMBE Divisions and Working Groups
  4. Reduced subscription to MBEC
  5. IFMBE News and calls for papers
  6. Representation at the IFMBE General Assembly through BESSA's delegates
BESSA in the IFMBE. BESSA's President, Prof Sudesh Sivarasu, serves on the IFMBE Administrative Council and co-chairs the IFMBE LMIC Working Group. BESSA's IFMBE delegate is Mr Mladen Poluta. BESSA circulates IFMBE calls for papers, elections and notices to its members and submits an annual membership return to the Federation.
