BESSA's flagship conference

SABEC 2027 — Southern Africa Biomedical Engineering Conference

11–12 March 2027 · UCT Graduate School of Business, Breakwater Campus, V&A Waterfront, Cape Town. Theme: "Biomedical Engineering Without Borders: Bridging Innovation and Access in Africa".

SABEC is BESSA's flagship scientific meeting, co-hosted with UCT MedTech and endorsed by the IFMBE. It brings together researchers, clinical engineers, clinicians, industry, regulators and students from across the region, with a Young Investigator Competition and up to ten scholarships for Master's and PhD students with accepted papers.
